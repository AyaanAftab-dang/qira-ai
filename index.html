What QIRA AI can do

QIRA AI is an assistant that reads your information, understands it, and helps you act on it. Give it your documents, spreadsheets, emails, tickets or live data, then ask for what you need in everyday language.

It doesn't just answer. It can also draft the reply, build the chart, or start the task, and it always shows where each answer came from so you can check it.

QIRA AI can make mistakes, so important decisions stay with you. Sensitive actions wait for your approval first.

What QIRA can do

Three core capabilities, designed to work on their own or together.

Understands context

Reads documents, tables, and conversations together, then answers with the full picture in mind.

Acts in real time

Streams results as data arrives and triggers actions the moment a threshold is crossed.

Private by design

Your data stays encrypted and is never used to train models you don't control.

How it works

From your first upload to a live answer takes minutes, not months.

Connect your data

Link files, databases, or apps. QIRA reads what's there and leaves the originals untouched.

Map the meaning

It finds the entities, relationships, and patterns in your data and builds a live knowledge graph.

Ask or automate

Ask questions in plain language, or set rules that run on their own when something changes.

Learn from feedback

Every correction you make tunes your private model, so results get sharper each week.

Trust and safety

Powerful AI only helps if you can rely on it. These principles shape every part of QIRA.

Sourced answers

Each response links back to the data behind it, so you can check the work in one click.

Human in control

Sensitive actions need approval. You set the limits, and every action is logged and reversible.

Tested constantly

Automated red-team tests run before each release to catch bias, errors, and misuse.
